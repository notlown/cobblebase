📢 **Cobblebase v1.3.8 — Major Update!**

---

🏭 **New: Producer Job**
~180 Pokemon now produce species-specific items! Wooloo makes wool, Miltank makes milk, Meowth drops gold nuggets, Trevenant produces dark oak logs, and much more. 35+ unique products across all categories.

🔧 **New: Admin GUI — Jobs Tab**
Server admins can now configure every job individually via `/cobblebase admin`:
- Custom cooldowns per job
- Radius min/max ranges
- Enable/disable jobs server-wide
- Disabled jobs auto-hide from player GUI

🧭 **Scout Improvements**
- Search radius massively increased (up to 1000 blocks at Prof 5)
- Xaero's Minimap waypoint integration — structure/biome discoveries get automatic waypoints
- Filters by spawn rarity instead of level
- Correct surface height for TP commands

🐛 **Bug Fixes**
- C2ME compatibility — no more threading crash
- Items from jobs now stack normally with vanilla items
- Pokemon no longer teleport ON TOP of the Pasture Block
- Stuck Pokemon redirect instead of teleporting (safe for pens/aquariums)
- Buffs tab only shows passive buffs when on Relax
- GUI shows correct assignments on first open
- Admin job config cooldowns now actually work

⚙️ **Config**
- Console logging toggle (default: off)
- Unstick threshold reduced to 7 seconds
